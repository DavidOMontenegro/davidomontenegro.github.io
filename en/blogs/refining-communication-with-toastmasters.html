Refining Communication with Toastmasters

Around that same time, I joined our company’s Toastmasters Club to overcome stage fright and improve my conversational skills (an area I really needed to work on). It quickly became one of the most rewarding experiences of my time here at Liferay. I formed relationships with talented, influential and all-around good people within the company, all while perfecting this critical and elusive skill we call "communication".


After being an active participant in the club for only 6 months, I was elected Vice President of Education. It was my job to coordinate sessions, mentor new members, and ensure everyone was involved, regularly giving speeches. I even got to host an open house event in our office to attract new participants along with Cláudio Souza and Anne Durey.


Toastmasters is great. People often overlook how critical public speaking is to their day-to-day work, but being a member and leader at this club gave me plenty of respect for the skill. While the soft skills aren't the ones I'm most passionate about (I love programming), it's hard to ignore the impact this had on my work. My dialogue is more fluid, my vocabulary has expanded, my filler words are at an all-time low and I convey information more clearly than ever.


If you're not sure that public speaking clubs like this actually work, I suggest you give it a try! I've received feedback that there's been noticeable improvement in my interpersonal communication from coworkers who weren't even aware of Toastmasters. It really works!


Additionally, my time at Toastmasters has helped me indirectly outside of my ordinary work at Liferay. I'd recommend anyone to speak confidently and authoritatively, build their own visibility, and learn to meet and speak to new people. These things advance your career more so than your actual performance. As an introvert, it's a bit tiring. But, it's so so worth it.


It was a pivotal step in my journey from a pure writing role to a more well-rounded developer advocate. It's a great pleasure to participate in Toastray and I hope I can continue to join our meetings and give more speeches! My only regret is not having been even more involved.
