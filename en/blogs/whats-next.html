What's Next?

Looking back on these past 2 years, I’m deeply grateful for the opportunities I’ve had, the teams I’ve worked with, and the many ways I’ve grown. Liferay was willing to take a chance on me despite my inexperience and they gave me all the tools I needed to excel. From QA testing and automation to documentation, public speaking, and cloud infrastructure, every chapter taught me something new about technology, communication, and teamwork. I’m excited to keep learning, keep building, and keep finding ways to add value wherever I can.


There's also a lot of non-work related events over these last 2 years I didn't have the opportunity to mention: I've moved, gotten married, had a child, and so much more. LinkedIn isn't really the place to go into depth on these things, but it's been a wild ride.


If you weren't already aware, in the middle of writing this blog series (between entry 5 and 6), I was let go from my job at Liferay. I hope these articles help paint a clear picture of all the good work I did so you won't think it was performance-based. However, this article isn't just sad news. I've already managed to find some web development freelance work. Also, more importantly, I'm happy to announce that, as of today, I have a new personal site on GitHub Pages!


The timing was impeccable since now I really need a way to share my portfolio. This personal site of mine will include blogs, personal projects, and even some games (though a lot of it is still in progress). Go check it out! I'll try to interact a little more often (I learned my lesson about not investing in myself after this surprise exit from my last job).


Before signing off, I'd just like to thank Liferay's CEO, Brian Chan. Thank you for giving me this job for 2 years and I'm honored you think I'm "a cheetah". I'd also like to thank the two managers I had during my time at Liferay. I couldn't have reached the heights I did without your trust and mentorship. I must also thank everyone on the documentation team and Toastmasters club. It was a pleasure working with you.
